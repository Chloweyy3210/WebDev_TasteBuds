About Taste Buds Catering

Started at a market stall. Built for every market stall.

We're a small Johannesburg-born team on a mission to make it easier for local food talent to be found — and for hosts to feed their guests something real.

Our story

Why we built this

Taste Buds Catering began in 2023 when co-founders Lindiwe Nkosi and Marco Rossi spent three exhausting weeks trying to book food trucks for a friend's wedding market. The trucks existed, the quality was outstanding, but nobody could actually find them — their contact details were scattered across Instagram comments, WhatsApp groups and word of mouth.

So they built the directory they wished existed: one place to browse vetted local caterers and food trucks, see real menus and prices, and message vendors directly. What started as a spreadsheet of twelve Johannesburg food trucks has grown into a platform used by hosts and vendors across four cities.

Where we're headed

Growing the local food scene

Every booking made through Taste Buds Catering puts money directly into an independent kitchen or truck, rather than a national chain. We're proud that most of our vendors are owner-operated businesses, many run by a single family or a two-person crew.

As we grow, our focus stays the same: make it effortless for hosts to find great local food, and make it effortless for small food businesses to be discovered — without needing their own website, marketing budget, or tech team.

What drives us

Mission, vision & who we serve

Mission

To connect local caterers and food trucks with the events that need them, so independent food businesses can grow without an advertising budget.

Vision

A future where every neighbourhood food truck and family caterer has the same online visibility as a national franchise.

Who we serve

Event hosts — from wedding planners to office managers — who want reliable local food, and vendors who want a storefront that gets them booked.
